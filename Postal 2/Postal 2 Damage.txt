Postal 2 damage

Headshots are determined based on the z coordinate of the shot. This means shots on the upper chest deal headshot damage.

Enemies lose a percentage of their health from close-range shots to the back of the head with the pistol. This portion is called "TakesPistolHeadShot" in the code.
-For many enemies, this value is 1.0, meaning these shots deal their entire maxhp worth of damage


Close-range shotgun headshots work the same way but with "TakesShotgunHeadShot." Damage is determined per pellet (it shoots 4 pellets), which is why they kill most enemies in 1 hit.
-Same damage per pellet for sawed-off, but triple the pellets
-There's actually a system for determining whether to use this or just multiply damage by 4 or just deal the enemy's entire max health. I do not understand the conditions.

Rifle headshots do the same with "TakesRifleHeadShot"
-It has no complicated system. It will always use TakesRifleHeadShot

Shovel headshots do the same with "TakesShovelHeadShot"
-headshots are random. Their chance is the same as the percent of health the enemy is missing. So an enemy at half health has a 50% chance to be headshotted. I do not believe the altfire can truly headshot.

Baton uses "TakesShotgunHeadShot"

	-You can tell if it headshots because their head is supposed to explode
