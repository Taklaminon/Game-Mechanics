All testing was done on mine ganados on difficulty 10, and it may vary with different enemy types.
HP is stored as a 16 bit signed integer. Many enemies, including bosses, have 1000 hp.

1 power = 127.5 damage (definitely varies based on enemy type)
This also varies based on difficulty. From 0-5 it's 300-30x. From 5-inf it's 172.5-4.5x.

Headshots deal 1.2x damage
All other body groups deal 1x damage


Knife
-Deals 220 damage but has no listed power
-Critical headshots deal 10x damage
-Crit rate is 0%
	-Much higher with exclusive handgun. Probably 35%

Handgun
-Critical headshots deal 10x damage
-Crit rate is around 8% (tested 100)
	-around 36% with exclusive (tested 200)

Broken Butterfly
-Critical headshots deal 25/9x damage (in general, crits deal damage in nice multiples of 5/9)
-Crit rate is low. Probably 10%

Bolt-Action Rifle
-Critical headshots deal 20/9x damage
-Crit rate is 100%

Striker
-Deals full damage even if only 1 pellet hits
-Seemingly always headshots if the pointer is on the head and never if the pointer is on the chest. Outside of that, it could either headshot or body shot, but I do not believe it is random.
-Critical headshots deal 75/9x damage 
-Crit rate is around 33% (tested)

TMP
-1.4x multiplier for damage (likely varies based on enemy)
-Critical headshots deal 10x damage
-Crit rate is around 5%

Mine Thrower
-has "power" of 20
-2 is no better than 1 due to invuln during knockback
-can headshot
	-always headshots if the dart lands on the head. Never headshots if the dart lands elsewhere on the body. Outside of that, it could either headshot or body shot, but I do not believe it is random.
-Splash damage deals same amount as direct hit.
-Critical headshots deal 30/9x damage. 
-Crit rate is low. Probably 7%


Standard crit rate seems to be 7%. Shotgun and exclusive pistol get 5x that. Rifle gets 100%.
