All testing was done on mine ganados, and it may vary with different enemy types.
HP is stored as a 16 bit signed integer.

1 power = 300 damage on ganados, Dr Salvador
1 power = 150 damage on soldiers
1 power = 60 damage on first gigante (unaffected by difficulty???)
1 power = 40 damage on regenerators
1 power = 30 damage on certain bosses (el gigante strong, Salazar, U3, Krauser)
1 power = 6 damage on Saddler
This also varies based on difficulty. From 0-5 it has a multiplier of 1-0.1x. From 5-inf it's 0.575-0.015x.
Each increase by 1 subtracts 10% of player damage until level 5. After level 5, it subtracts 1.5%.

Headshots deal 1.2x damage
All other body groups deal 1x damage

Dr Salvador cannot be critically headshotted
-Damage to him is capped at 2000 per shot. This is before difficulty is applied, so it's equivalent to 6.66 power.

Regenerators cannot be headshotted
-shots on plagas always do 1000 damage, regardless of difficulty (Regenerator hp is their number of plagas times 1000)
	-Iron Maidens work the same
-Only rifles can destroy plagas. Magnums cannot.

Salazar cannot be headshotted

U3 cannot be headshotted
-He seems to have a "stun bar" that depletes whenever he is hit. This stun bar seems to be set to a random value after he is stunned.
	-He always stuns in 1-2 shotgun shells, regardless of damage
	-He always stuns in 10-12 TMP shots
	-He always stuns in 1 magnum shot
-He will not stun while on 1 hp.
-If he is brought to 1 hp during idle or during an attack, he will jump away.
-I am not sure whether he will jump away if he is brought to 1 hp while jumping to a container.
-He always jumps from the ceiling with 500 hp. You actually can damage him while he's swinging at you from the ceiling, and it will make his next drop easier.
	-He starts container 2 with 4500 hp, but his health will be immediately reset to 500 the next time he jumps to the ceiling.

Krauser headshots deal 2x damage
-cannot be crit headshotted
-has 10000 hp

Saddler
-Shots on leg eyes deal 2x damage
-Ada will throw a rocket launcher to Leon when Saddler falls to 4000 hp (half health).
-Saddler's death is not determined by his health. It's determined by the health of a separate entity created just to track his actual health.
	-This entity is damaged every time Saddler is damaged, but it's also damaged at a rate of 1 power = 30 damage (capped at 16.66 power) whenever Saddler's mouth eye is hit.
	-After the mouth eye takes on 26.66 power (pre-cap), it will immediately close. This is also the amount the eye stab deals. The player can shoot with Striker twice and then stab for 50.666 power.
	-Saddler's health will snap to that of the entity when his body is shot

Knife
-Has a power between 1.72666 and 1.73
-30.2666-30.3 against Krauser
-Critical headshots deal 10x damage
-Crit rate is 0%
	-Much higher with exclusive handgun. Probably 35%

Handgun
-Critical headshots deal 10x damage
-Crit rate is around 8% (tested 100)
	-around 36% with exclusive (tested 200)

Broken Butterfly
-0.5x damage to U3
-Critical headshots deal 25/9x damage (in general, crits deal damage in nice multiples of 5/9)
-Crit rate is low. Probably 10%

Bolt-Action Rifle
-Critical headshots deal 20/9x damage
-Crit rate is 100%

Striker
-Deals full damage even if only 1 pellet hits
-Seemingly always headshots if the pointer is on the head and never if the pointer is on the chest. Outside of that, it could either headshot or body shot, but I do not believe it is random.
-Critical headshots deal 75/9x damage 
-Crit rate is around 33% (tested)

TMP
-1.4x multiplier for damage for ganados. 0.7x multiplier for Salvador. 0.8x for salazar/U3/Krauser/Saddler. This indicates that each enemy might have different weaknesses and resistances.
-Critical headshots deal 10x damage
-Crit rate is around 5%

Mine Thrower
-has "power" of 20
-2 is no better than 1 due to invuln during knockback
-can headshot
	-always headshots if the dart lands on the head. Never headshots if the dart lands elsewhere on the body. Outside of that, it could either headshot or body shot, but I do not believe it is random.
-Splash damage deals same amount as direct hit.
-Critical headshots deal 30/9x damage. 
-Crit rate is low. Probably 7%

Grenade
-has "power" of 15
	-yea, from my testing, it's worse than the mine darts

Incendiary
-has "power" of 33.33 for U3, but possibly different for other enemies

Barrel
-has "power" of 16.66 for U3

Standard crit rate seems to be 7%. Shotgun and exclusive pistol get 5x that. Rifle gets 100%.
