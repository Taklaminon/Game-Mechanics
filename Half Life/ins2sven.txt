pistols:
VP70 is the most competent 9mm pistol
	Damage is the same across guns using the same ammo
Match is the most competent 45 ACP pistol
	match has better eco, but VP70 is probably better for survivability (and has less overkill)
makarov and mauser aren't that good for eco. They also just aren't that good. Makarov has a place since it's only 100 dollars, but mauser is too expensive for its ammo cost and poor performance.
deagle is dogshit for eco. Just buy a revolver honestly

revolvers:
webley is very practical and is much better for eco than any pistol. Plus it gibs.
python has worse dps but better damage per shot. still worse eco. don't buy it.
model 29 has an absurdly long reload and is worse eco. It doesn't even gib. Don't buy it.

carbines:
schnell really sucks, ammo is too expensive and the ironsights are bad
Mk. 18 has great sights, but has 10% worse damage than M4A1
M4A1 is one of two eco choices. It uses the same ammo as Mk.18 but does 10% more damage, iron sights aren't so bad
G36C, AKS-74U are both just worse M4A1s
M1A1 is semi-auto and has a bayonet. It's not very good as a gun.
SKS is semi-auto, it's another eco choice along with M4A1. It's only slightly better at eco and has annoying ironsights, so just use M4A1

the actually good carbines are all better eco than the match but a lot worse than the webley
