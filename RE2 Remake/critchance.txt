How to calculate crit rate:

Fire a large number of shots
Find the amount of damage dealt along with the number of shots
Find damage dealt on a crit and damage dealt on a normal shot

totalDamage = shots*cRate*cDamage + shots*(1-cRate)*nDamage
totalDamage/shots = cRate*cDamage + nDamage - cRate*nDamage
cRate * cDamage - cRate*nDamage = totalDamage/shots - nDamage
cRate * (cDamage - nDamage) = totalDamage/shots - nDamage
cRate = (totalDamage/shots - nDamage)/(cDamage-nDamage)

crit rate in your sample will be (the average damage dealt minus the damage of a normal shot) divided by the difference between critical and normal damage.
